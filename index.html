⚙️ Vehicle & Fuel Settings

The default mileage is set to 60 km/L. Enter the current petrol price. If you change the price, fuel expenses for all days will be recalculated using the new price.

➕ Add Daily Entry

Enter the actual number of hours you worked or drove that day. Your shift can be different every day: enter 4, 5, 6, 4.5, or any other duration. The litres of petrol you fill are recorded separately. Profit is calculated using distance travelled ÷ mileage × petrol price. Profit per hour = daily profit ÷ working hours. This avoids counting the full amount you paid at the pump as that day’s fuel expense when some fuel remains in the tank.
